Question for all:

Can you formally solve the following problem:   Let N be a natural number and let 
u_i >= 0 between 1 and N. We set u_0 = u_N and u_{N+1} = u_1. The claim is
$\sum_{i = 1}N u_i(u_{i - 2}^2 - 4u_{i - 1}^2 + 3u_i^2) >= 0$
(I tried to express that in LaTeX code)
 Please give a proof or a counterexample.

Note: Lean and Mathematica don't understand questions in natural language, and for beginners it is a non-trivial task to translate the question in their language.
